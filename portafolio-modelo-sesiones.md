# Portafolio de modelo: sesiones de fotos para Flow + Nano Banana

Doce sesiones completas para armar un *book* de modelo profesional. Cada una tiene concepto, estilismo, luz y una **lista de tomas** (portada, cuerpo entero, primer plano, movimiento y detalle), como en una producción real.

Los prompts están en inglés porque el modelo los sigue mejor. Las explicaciones están en español.

---

## 0. Configuración (hazla una vez)

### Qué es cada referencia

Sube las 6 imágenes en este orden y nómbralas así en tu cabeza:

| # | Imagen | Para qué sirve |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Cara de frente | Identidad |
| 2 | Cara 3/4 | Identidad, estructura ósea |
| 3 | Cara de perfil / otra expresión | Identidad, pelo |
| 4 | Cuerpo entero | Altura, complexión y proporciones |
| 5 | Chaqueta marrón puesta | Prenda propia (cómo te queda) |
| 6 | Chaqueta / prenda extendida | Prenda propia (textura, color, detalles) |

> Si en alguna sesión **no** quieres tu ropa, quita las imágenes 5 y 6 de las referencias. Si no, el modelo tiende a meter la chaqueta en todo.

### Bloques fijos (cópialos al inicio de cada prompt)

**`ID LOCK`**: va siempre.
```
Reference images 1-3 define the model's exact identity: keep his face, bone structure, eye shape, thick dark eyebrows, nose, lips, jawline, light stubble and skin tone identical. Keep his signature hair: dark brown, voluminous, wavy shaggy wolf-cut with curtain bangs and flipped-out ends. Reference image 4 defines his height, build and body proportions; do not make him more muscular or taller. No beautification, no age change. Professional model photography, natural skin texture, real pores, sharp eyes.
```

**`WARDROBE LOCK`**: solo en las sesiones que usan tu ropa.
```
Reference images 5-6 show his own brown jacket: reproduce its exact color, material, texture, collar, seams, buttons/zipper and fit as seen in the references.
```

### Reglas para que parezca una sesión real

1. **Genera primero la toma de portada** de cada sesión. Cuando tengas una buena, **súmala como referencia** para las demás tomas de esa sesión. Así se mantienen la luz, el color y el estilismo.
2. **Formatos de book**: `4:5` o `2:3` vertical para casi todo y `3:2` horizontal para 1 o 2 tomas por sesión (los books necesitan algunas apaisadas para las dobles páginas).
3. Si la cara se aleja de la tuya, vuelve a generar sumando: `face must match references 1-3 exactly`.
4. Si se ve "demasiado IA", suma: `shot on medium format film, subtle grain, slight imperfections, real fabric wrinkles`.

### ⚠️ Importante: digitales reales

Las agencias **siempre** piden *digitales* (o polaroids): fotos reales, sin retoque, con luz natural. Úsalas para ver cómo eres de verdad. **Esas tómalas con el celular, no con IA**: si llegas a un casting y no te pareces a lo que mandaste, se pierde la confianza. Las imágenes de este pack sirven para mostrar **rango, actitud y concepto** en tu web, Instagram o *moodboard*. Si un cliente o agencia pregunta, di con claridad que son conceptos generados.

**Guía rápida para tus digitales reales**: pared blanca, luz de ventana, camiseta negra o blanca ajustada, jeans oscuros, sin accesorios. Tomas: frente, 3/4, perfil izquierdo, perfil derecho, cuerpo entero frente, cuerpo entero perfil y una sonriendo.

## SESIÓN 1 · «Cuero y concreto»
**Usa tu ropa.** Moda masculina urbana en arquitectura brutalista, conectada con el edificio de tus fotos.
**Estilismo**: tu chaqueta marrón, cuello alto negro fino, pantalón negro recto y botas Chelsea negras.
**Luz**: día nublado, suave, con sombras limpias. Paleta gris + marrón cálido.

**Base de sesión** (pégala después de los locks):
```
[ID LOCK] [WARDROBE LOCK] Menswear editorial on location at a raw-concrete brutalist building. Styling: his own brown jacket, thin black turtleneck, straight black trousers, black leather Chelsea boots. Soft overcast daylight, muted grey concrete with warm brown accent, medium format film look, 80mm lens, quiet confident attitude, editorial magazine quality.
```
**Tomas:**
- **Portada**: `Waist-up, leaning his shoulder against a massive concrete column, jacket collar popped, looking directly into the lens with a calm intense gaze. 4:5.`
- **Cuerpo entero**: `Full-body shot, walking down wide concrete stairs toward camera, one hand in trouser pocket, mid-stride, symmetrical architecture behind. 2:3.`
- **Apaisada (doble página)**: `Extreme wide shot, he is small, sitting on the edge of a concrete ledge with legs hanging, vast geometric facade filling the frame. 3:2.`
- **Primer plano**: `Tight portrait, chin slightly down, eyes up, a strand of hair falling over one eye, leather texture of the collar visible in the foreground. 4:5.`
- **Detalle**: `Close-up of his hand adjusting the jacket zipper/button, rings-free, concrete blurred behind, focus on leather grain. 4:5.`

## SESIÓN 2 · «Color Block» (estudio de alta moda)
Fondo continuo de color saturado. Es la sesión que más "agencia" se ve.
**Estilismo**: traje oversize del mismo color que el fondo (efecto tono sobre tono), sin camisa o con camiseta de tirantes blanca.
**Luz**: *beauty dish* con sombra dura y definida.

```
[ID LOCK] High-fashion studio editorial. Seamless paper backdrop in [cobalt blue | tomato red | butter yellow | sage green]. He wears an oversized double-breasted suit in the exact same color as the backdrop (tone-on-tone), white ribbed tank top underneath, black square-toe shoes. Hard beauty-dish key light with crisp shadow on the backdrop, clean high-end retouching but natural skin, Vogue Hommes editorial attitude.
```
**Tomas:**
- **Portada**: `Seated backwards on a simple chrome chair, arms crossed over the backrest, chin resting on his arms, direct gaze. 4:5.`
- **Cuerpo entero**: `Full-body, angular pose, one knee bent, jacket open, hand pushing hair back, strong shadow cast on the backdrop. 2:3.`
- **Movimiento**: `Mid-jump, jacket flaring open, hair lifted, frozen with high-speed flash. 4:5.`
- **Variación**: genera la misma toma en los 4 colores y elige 2 para el book (una fría y una cálida).

## SESIÓN 3 · «Pelo» (beauty / grooming)
Tu pelo es tu sello. Esta sesión lo pone al frente y sirve para castings de marcas de cuidado del pelo y grooming.
**Luz**: suave y envolvente de estudio, fondo gris medio.

```
[ID LOCK] Men's beauty and grooming campaign, studio. Mid-grey seamless background, soft large octabox light, subtle rim light separating his hair from the background. Bare shoulders or plain white t-shirt. Focus on the texture, volume and movement of his wavy wolf-cut hair. Ultra-sharp 100mm macro-portrait quality, natural skin, clean and premium.
```
**Tomas:**
- **Viento**: `Wind machine from the front-left, hair blown back dramatically revealing his forehead, eyes closed, serene. 4:5.`
- **Mojado**: `Hair soaking wet slicked back with water droplets running down his face and neck, intense gaze, glossy skin. 4:5.`
- **Manos**: `Both hands running through his hair from the forehead back, elbows up, looking slightly down. 4:5.`
- **Perfil**: `Pure side profile, hair falling naturally, clean silhouette against grey. 4:5.`
- **Sacudida**: `Shaking his head, hair in motion blur while his face stays sharp, slow-sync flash. 4:5.`

## SESIÓN 4 · «Blanco y negro, piel real»
Retrato monocromo tipo *fine art*, sin estilismo pesado. Es la sesión que más pesa en un book porque muestra la cara pura.
**Estilismo**: camisa blanca de algodón abierta y arrugada, o camiseta negra.

```
[ID LOCK] Black-and-white fine-art fashion portrait, high-contrast silver gelatin print look, deep blacks and luminous highlights, visible film grain. Natural window light from one side, no makeup look, raw honest skin texture, minimal styling: a creased open white cotton shirt. Timeless 1990s supermodel-era photography mood.
```
**Tomas:**
- **Portada**: `Extreme close-up filling the frame from eyebrows to chin, one eye in light, one in shadow. 4:5.`
- **Sentado**: `Sitting on the floor against a white wall, knees up, forearms resting on knees, looking off-frame, contemplative. 4:5.`
- **Sonrisa**: `Genuine laugh caught mid-moment, head tilted back slightly, eyes crinkled. 4:5.` *(los books necesitan al menos una sonrisa real)*
- **Playa** (variante exterior): `Windy beach, overcast, shirt blowing open, hair across face, wide 3:2 frame with empty horizon.`

## SESIÓN 5 · «Sastrería italiana»
Campaña de traje clásico. Demuestra que también sirves para marcas de lujo formal.
**Estilismo**: traje cruzado color tabaco o gris carbón, corbata de seda, mocasines y gafas de carey.

```
[ID LOCK] Luxury Italian menswear campaign. Location: an old Roman palazzo with faded frescoed walls, marble floor and tall shuttered windows letting in warm late-afternoon light beams. He wears a perfectly tailored [tobacco brown | charcoal grey] double-breasted suit, cream shirt, dark silk tie, brown suede loafers, tortoiseshell sunglasses. Warm golden color grade, elegant, sophisticated, slightly nonchalant attitude, shot on medium format.
```
**Tomas:**
- **Portada**: `Standing in a beam of window light, adjusting his cufflink, looking at camera from under his bangs. 4:5.`
- **Cuerpo entero**: `Full body leaning on a marble balustrade, legs crossed at the ankle, sunglasses in hand. 2:3.`
- **Apaisada**: `Sitting alone at the end of a long empty dining table with an espresso, wide symmetrical 3:2 composition.`
- **Exterior**: `Walking across a sunlit cobblestone piazza holding his jacket over his shoulder, Vespa blurred in background. 4:5.`

## SESIÓN 6 · «Comercial / lifestyle»
**La más importante para ganar dinero.** Las agencias comerciales (ropa casual, tecnología, bancos, bebidas) necesitan verte sonriendo, natural y cercano. Muchos books de chicos con tu estilo no la tienen.
**Estilismo**: denim claro, camiseta blanca, camisa de lino y sneakers blancos.

```
[ID LOCK] Commercial lifestyle campaign photography, bright and warm natural light, approachable and genuine, real candid moments, clean modern color grade, authentic smile. Styling: light-wash denim, crisp white t-shirt, open linen shirt in [sky blue | sand], clean white sneakers.
```
**Tomas:**
- **Café**: `Sitting at a sunny café window table, laughing at something off-camera, holding a ceramic cup with both hands. 4:5.`
- **Bici**: `Riding a vintage city bicycle down a tree-lined street, golden hour, looking over his shoulder smiling. 4:5.`
- **Tecnología**: `Sitting on a couch in a bright minimalist apartment, holding a phone, reacting with a surprised happy expression, headphones around neck. 4:5.`
- **Denim**: `Full-body against a sunlit pastel wall, thumbs in jean pockets, relaxed smile, shadow of palm leaves. 2:3.`

## SESIÓN 7 · «Fragancia» (nocturna, cinematográfica)
Las campañas de perfume son las más aspiracionales. Hay agua, noche, misterio y piel.

```
[ID LOCK] Luxury men's fragrance campaign, cinematic, dark and sensual but elegant. Night, deep blue and black palette with a single warm light source. Water elements, mist, glossy reflections. Minimal styling: open black silk shirt. Shot like a high-end perfume TV commercial still, anamorphic lens, subtle film grain.
```
**Tomas:**
- **Mar**: `Emerging from a dark night sea up to the chest, water streaming from his hair, moonlight reflecting on the wet skin, gaze directly at camera. 4:5.`
- **Vidrio**: `Behind a glass panel covered in water droplets, his face partially revealed through a wiped streak, warm amber light. 4:5.`
- **Coche**: `Sitting in the back seat of a car at night, city lights streaking across his face through the window, looking out. 3:2.`
- **Frasco** (toma de producto): `Close-up of his hand holding an unbranded square glass perfume bottle near his collarbone, shallow focus. 4:5.`

## SESIÓN 8 · «Lookbook nocturno de calle»
**Usa tu ropa.** Streetwear con flash directo en la ciudad de noche. Conecta con lo que se ve en Instagram.

Direct on-camera flash mixed with neon ambient, slight motion trails, raw editorial street style.

- **Paso**: `Crossing a zebra crossing mid-stride, blurred crowd around him with slow shutter, he is sharp. 2:3.`
- **Kiosco**: `Leaning against a vending machine glowing in the dark, its light on his face, eating something casually. 4:5.`
- **Metro**: `Inside an empty metro car, sitting with legs spread, one arm over the seat back, fluorescent light. 3:2.`

## SESIÓN 9 · «Atlético al amanecer»
Para marcas deportivas o de *athleisure*. Muestra energía y cuerpo en movimiento, sin exagerar la musculatura.

```
[ID LOCK] Sportswear campaign at dawn on an empty athletics track, mist on the ground, soft pink and blue sky, backlit sun flare. Styling: technical black running jacket, grey running shorts over black tights, performance sneakers. Dynamic, cinematic sports photography, keep his natural lean build from reference 4.
```
**Tomas:**
- **Sprint**: `Mid-sprint toward camera, low angle, hair flying, sweat on forehead, focused expression. 4:5.`
- **Descanso**: `Sitting on the track lane line catching his breath, elbows on knees, looking at the sunrise. 3:2.`
- **Retrato**: `Tight portrait with a towel around his neck, sunlight rim on his hair, slight smile. 4:5.`

## SESIÓN 10 · «Escultura» (conceptual / avant-garde)
Muestra que puedes trabajar con diseñadores experimentales. Son las imágenes más arriesgadas y memorables del book.

```
[ID LOCK] Avant-garde conceptual fashion editorial, art-gallery aesthetic, white minimalist space, museum lighting. He wears sculptural designer pieces. Serious, statuesque posing, body as sculpture. Medium format, ultra clean.
```
**Tomas (elige 2–3):**
- `Wrapped in an enormous cloud of white tulle that extends across the floor, only his face and one hand visible. 4:5.`
- `Wearing a structured paper-like white coat with exaggerated geometric shoulders, standing on a plinth like a statue. 2:3.`
- `Tangled in long strips of red fabric suspended from the ceiling, mid-motion, frozen. 4:5.`
- `Silhouette behind a backlit translucent sheet, his hair outline sharp, only shadow visible. 4:5.`
- `Covered in fine silver body shimmer, bare shoulders, classical Greek-statue pose, cool white light. 4:5.`

Bright clean light, medium format, magazine cover quality.

## Cómo armar el book final (15–20 imágenes)

Un book bien ordenado vale más que muchas fotos buenas sin orden.

| Posición | Imagen | Sesión |
|---|---|---|
| 1 (portada) | Primer plano más fuerte | S4 blanco y negro o S3 viento |
| 2 | Cuerpo entero limpio | S2 Color Block |
| 3–4 | Editorial con tu ropa | S1 concreto (portada + cuerpo entero) |
| 5 | Sonrisa comercial | S6 café |
| 6 | Apaisada (doble página) | S5 palazzo o S1 apaisada |
| 7–8 | Beauty | S3 mojado + manos |
| 9 | Noche | S7 mar o S8 neón |
| 10 | Deporte | S9 sprint |
| 11 | Formal | S5 portada |
| 12–13 | Conceptual | S10 (2 imágenes) |
| 14 | Pasarela | S11 |
| 15 | Firma personal | S12 Aero Couture |
| 16 | Cierre | S4 sonrisa blanco y negro |

**Checklist de rango** (un agente busca esto en 30 segundos):
- [ ] Cara pura sin estilismo (S3, S4)
- [ ] Cuerpo entero (S1, S2, S6)
- [ ] Sonrisa natural (S4, S6)
- [ ] Formal (S5) y casual (S6, S8)
- [ ] Movimiento (S2, S9, S11)
- [ ] Algo arriesgado (S10, S12)
- [ ] Color y blanco y negro

**Consejos finales**
- Alterna en el book **color, blanco y negro, interior y exterior**. Dos fotos parecidas seguidas se restan valor.
- Mantén tu pelo **igual en todas**. Es tu marca: si cambia, el book parece de varias personas.
- Para Instagram profesional: publica cada sesión como carrusel de 4–5 fotos con un título corto en el pie (ej.: *"Cuero y concreto — SS27 concept"*).
