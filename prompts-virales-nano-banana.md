# Prompts virales para Flow + Nano Banana (con tus fotos como base)

Pack de prompts para Instagram y TikTok, pensado para usar tus selfies como **referencia de identidad**.
Los prompts van en **inglés** porque el modelo los sigue con más precisión; las explicaciones van en español.

---

## 0. Cómo usarlos (léelo antes, cambia mucho el resultado)

1. **Sube 2 o 3 fotos tuyas** como *ingredientes* o referencias: una de frente, una de 3/4 y una de perfil (las tres que ya tienes sirven).
2. **Pega siempre el bloque `IDENTITY LOCK`** al principio de cada prompt. Es lo que evita que te cambie la cara o el pelo.
3. **Formato**: `4:5` para el feed de Instagram, `9:16` para Reels, TikTok e historias. `3:4` si vas a hacer un carrusel.
4. **Genera 4 resultados por prompt** y quédate con el mejor. Si la cara se aleja de la tuya, vuelve a generar sumando la frase: *"face must match reference exactly"*.
5. **Para video en Flow**: genera primero la imagen, úsala como *frame inicial* y añade el prompt de movimiento (`MOTION`) que va debajo de varios de los prompts.
6. Cambia lo que está entre `[corchetes]` para crear tus propias variantes.

### IDENTITY LOCK (copia y pega al inicio de todo)

```
Use the uploaded reference photos as the exact identity of the subject. Keep his face, bone structure, eye shape, thick dark eyebrows, nose, lips, light stubble and skin tone 100% identical. Keep his signature hair: dark brown, voluminous, wavy shaggy wolf-cut / mullet with curtain bangs and flipped-out ends. Do not beautify, do not change age, do not change ethnicity. Photorealistic, natural skin texture with visible pores.
```

## 1. FRUTIGER AERO ✦ (el set estrella)

Estética 2004–2012: cielos azules imposibles, burbujas, agua cristalina, vidrio brillante, peces tropicales, hierba ultra verde, interfaces de Windows Vista / Wii / iPod, todo glossy y optimista. Ahora mismo vuelve fuerte con el *nostalgiacore* y el *Y2K tardío*.

**Paleta**: azul cielo, aqua, verde lima, blanco perla, cromado.
**Truco para destacar**: la mayoría de la gente hace Frutiger Aero solo con fondos. Tú mételo **en la ropa, la cámara y la acción**.

### 1.1 «Desktop Wallpaper Human»
```
[IDENTITY LOCK] Frutiger Aero aesthetic. The subject is standing waist-deep in impossibly clear turquoise water on a perfect green grassy hill under a glossy cerulean sky with fluffy white cumulus clouds, as if he lives inside a 2007 Windows Vista default wallpaper. Giant iridescent soap bubbles float around him, one bubble reflecting his face. He wears a translucent glossy aqua raincoat over a white tank top, small silver chain. Shot on a wide 24mm lens from slightly below, lens flare, aurora light streaks, hyper-saturated greens and blues, glossy plastic sheen, clean and optimistic mood, 2000s CGI-meets-photo look. 4:5.
```
`MOTION:` *Slow dolly in, bubbles drift upward, clouds move fast in timelapse, water ripples around his waist, he slowly looks up at a bubble.*

### 1.2 «Aquarium Head» (concepto surrealista viral)
```
[IDENTITY LOCK] Frutiger Aero surreal portrait. The subject's head and shoulders are inside a giant perfectly clear glass sphere filled with crystal water, small tropical clownfish and blue tangs swimming around his hair, his wavy hair floating weightlessly underwater. He keeps his eyes open with a calm, deadpan expression. Bright sky-blue gradient background with white light rays and glossy green leaves. Studio beauty lighting, caustic light patterns on his face, high-gloss reflections on the glass, ultra sharp, editorial. 4:5.
```
`MOTION:` *Fish orbit his head, his hair sways slowly underwater, a single air bubble escapes from his lips and rises.*

### 1.3 «Wii Lobby / Mii Channel»
```
[IDENTITY LOCK] Frutiger Aero meets Nintendo Wii menu. The subject sits cross-legged floating in a soft white-and-light-blue glossy void with a subtle grid floor, surrounded by giant floating rounded-square glass app icons (weather, music, globe, fish tank) that reflect him. He wears an oversized white puffer jacket with baby-blue piping, white wide cargo pants, chunky silver-and-white sneakers, holding a white retro game controller. Soft diffused light, glossy reflections, pastel aqua accents, clean 2008 UI aesthetic, playful but cool expression. 9:16.
```
`MOTION:` *Icons rotate slowly around him like a carousel, subtle floating bob, the camera orbits 90 degrees.*

### 1.4 «Eco Utopia 2008 Ad»
```
[IDENTITY LOCK] Frutiger Aero eco-futurism advertising photo. The subject stands on a glass rooftop garden of a white curved futuristic eco-building, with wind turbines, lush ferns, a waterfall flowing down the glass, and a bright blue sky. He holds a translucent green glass sphere containing a tiny tree. Outfit: white technical windbreaker, sage green trousers, transparent visor sunglasses with blue tint pushed up into his hair. Low-angle hero shot, wide lens, sunburst behind his head, bloom effect, hyper-clean, 2008 corporate optimism. Add a subtle glossy chrome logo-free badge on his chest. 4:5.
```

```
[IDENTITY LOCK] Extreme fisheye selfie POV from inside a giant transparent soap bubble that the subject is holding with both hands at arm's length; the bubble's iridescent rainbow film distorts and frames everything. [IDENTITY LOCK] Close-up portrait framed as a glossy 2007 instant-messenger avatar: rounded square frame with a thick translucent aqua glass border and a green "online" orb in the corner. ## 2. BRUTALISMO EDITORIAL (aprovecha el edificio de tu fondo)
```

Encaja con tu look y con el fondo de concreto de tus fotos. El contraste *chico de pelo salvaje + concreto gigante* funciona muy bien en el feed.

### 2.1 «Tiny human, giant concrete»
```
[IDENTITY LOCK] Fashion editorial. Extreme wide shot from far below: the subject is tiny, lying on his back on a massive raw-concrete brutalist staircase, arms spread, looking up at the camera. Overcast soft light, monochrome grey palette with one color accent: he wears an oversized cherry-red leather jacket, black flared trousers and pointed boots. Medium format film, 35mm, symmetrical composition, minimal, Kinfolk x Dazed magazine style. 4:5.
```

### 2.2 «Wind machine»
```
[IDENTITY LOCK] Fashion campaign shot against a brutalist concrete building facade. A powerful wind blows from the side, his wavy hair whipping dramatically across his face, a long black trench coat flying horizontally like a cape. He leans into the wind at an impossible 30-degree angle. Shot on 50mm with hard flash mixed with grey daylight, high contrast, sharp, editorial, Balenciaga campaign energy. 4:5.
```
`MOTION:` *Strong wind gusts, coat and hair flutter wildly, slow motion 120 fps feel.*

### 2.3 «Escalera infinita (Escher)»
```
[IDENTITY LOCK] Surreal architectural photo: the subject walks up an endless Escher-like brutalist concrete staircase that loops on itself, three copies of him seen at different points of the loop — one walking up, one sitting, one upside down on the ceiling stairs. Same outfit in all three: grey knit sweater, black wide trousers, white socks, black loafers. Cool overcast light, perfect symmetry, ultra sharp. 4:5.
```

## 3. CÁMARAS ORIGINALES (lo que más para el scroll)

El ángulo de cámara es lo que más diferencia un post. Aquí, una toma por tipo de cámara.

### 3.1 Fisheye skate (Y2K / 2000s skate video)
```
[IDENTITY LOCK] Extreme fisheye lens shot from the ground, the subject jumping over the camera on a skateboard mid-kickflip, the board and his sneaker very close to the lens, his hair flying, sky behind. Wears baggy dark jeans, an oversized vintage band-style tee with no readable text, a beanie in his hand. 2003 skate video look, VX1000 camcorder vibe, slight motion blur, harsh afternoon sun, high contrast. 9:16.
```

### 3.2 Paparazzi con flash duro
```
[IDENTITY LOCK] Paparazzi photo at night: the subject leaving a restaurant, caught mid-step, raising one hand halfway to cover his face but still visible, slight smirk. Harsh direct on-camera flash, background dark with car lights bokeh, red-eye-free, slightly tilted framing. Outfit: long black leather coat, white tank top, silver chain, small sunglasses on the tip of his nose. 2000s tabloid photo style, grain. 4:5.
```

### 3.3 Cámara de seguridad (CCTV)
```
[IDENTITY LOCK] Security camera footage still from a high corner angle in a 24-hour convenience store at 3am: the subject looks directly up into the camera while holding a carton of milk and a bouquet of flowers. Fluorescent green-tinted lighting, low resolution, timestamp overlay "03:14:07" and "CAM 02" in the corner, slight fisheye distortion, scan lines. Mysterious, cinematic. 4:3.
```

### 3.4 Desde dentro de la lavadora / nevera / buzón (POV objeto)
```
[IDENTITY LOCK] POV shot from inside a [washing machine | refrigerator | mailbox], the subject leaning down and peering in with curiosity, his face lit by the [blue interior light | warm fridge light]. The opening frames his face in a circle/rectangle, everything else dark. Wide lens, shallow depth of field, cinematic, playful. 9:16.
```

### 3.5 Toma cenital tumbado en algo inesperado
```
[IDENTITY LOCK] Top-down overhead shot: the subject lies on his back in a [pile of thousands of oranges | ball pit of white balls | bed of blue hydrangeas | pile of vinyl records], only his face, shoulders and one hand visible, deadpan expression. Soft even daylight, rich color, perfectly composed, fine-art photography, 4:5.
```

### 3.6 Reflejo en una cuchara / gota / ojo

```
[IDENTITY LOCK] Macro photo of a polished silver spoon; in its convex reflection we see the subject's distorted face up close, he is holding the spoon and looking into it. The real background is softly blurred warm kitchen light. Ultra detailed, creative, product-photography sharpness. 4:5.
```

**Tips para más vistas**
- La **primera slide** de cada carrusel tiene que ser la más rara (1.2 o 3.4), no la más bonita.
- En TikTok, pon texto en pantalla los primeros 2 segundos: *"le pedí a la IA que me metiera en un fondo de Windows Vista"*.
- Hashtags del set Frutiger Aero: `#frutigeraero #y2kaesthetic #nostalgiacore #aeroaesthetic #windowsvista #2000score`.
- Mantén un **detalle tuyo constante** en todas (la cadena plateada, por ejemplo): crea una identidad de marca visual.
- Si alguna imagen sale demasiado perfecta, añade *"slight imperfections, candid, natural skin"*: lo "demasiado IA" se scrollea más rápido.
